# Outreach Email Templates

These are working templates for prospect and contact outreach. They should be adapted to the person, company, source and current requirement before sending. Nothing is auto-sent.

## 1. Recruiter / Technology Partner — Ecommerce Project Support

**Use when:** A recruiter, technology consultancy, development agency or systems partner works with clients who may need ecommerce operational/domain support.

**To:** [Verified recipient email]\n\n**Subject:** Ecommerce Operations & Systems Project Support

Hi [First name],

I spotted [the role/post/requirement] you're recruiting for and it led me to have a look at what [Company] does.

There could be a good crossover. I work with ecommerce businesses from the **pain point backwards** — find out what's actually going wrong, follow the operation end-to-end, then design a practical workflow around the systems the client already has, without needing to replace or interfere with them.

That can run across Shopify, marketplaces, inventory, 3PL/warehouse, orders, fulfilment, shipping, reconciliation and costs. I can work independently or alongside your/client's existing team, and where development or integration is needed, work with the technical team to turn the operational requirement into something practical.

I've got my own office and working structure, so I can take on and execute projects remotely.

If that could add something to the projects your team comes across, I'd be happy to have a chat by phone or email.

Kind regards,  
**Bhavin Tailor**  
Independent Ecommerce Operations & Systems Consultant  
07774 257009

### Rules for this template

- Do not mention BT38 unless Bhavin specifically wants it included.
- Position Bhavin as available to work **with the agency/company**, independently, or alongside its client's team.
- Remote/project-based work is the target.
- Emphasise designing workflows around the client's existing systems rather than automatically replacing systems.
- Keep the pain-point-backwards wording where appropriate.
- If the company develops or integrates systems, make the crossover explicit: Bhavin establishes the operational requirement; technical teams can build/integrate where required.
- Keep the email concise and conversational rather than turning it into a CV.
- **Company-specific drafting is mandatory:** before writing an outreach email, research how the recipient's company actually operates, what services/markets it specialises in, who its clients are, and why Bhavin is relevant to that specific model.
- Mention the company's relevant specialisms naturally in the email where they create a genuine crossover. For recruitment firms, this includes the disciplines/markets they recruit for (for example Ecommerce, Amazon Marketplace, DTC, Shopify or other marketplace roles when verified).
- Do not send a generic template with only the company/contact name changed. The template is a structure; the final email must be tailored to the researched company.
- Adapt the channel list to the actual opportunity; do not claim specialist expertise that has not been established.
- End with an easy phone-or-email conversation.
- Every prepared email must include a **To:** field with the verified recipient email address and a **Subject:** line. Never leave either out.\n- Verify the email from the prospect/company source or official website before presenting it; do not guess an address.\n- Every finished email must be delivered in a **one-click copy/paste-ready format** with To, Subject and the complete body together; no assembly should be required.
- Default sender account for approved email outreach: **bhavinrtailor@gmail.com**.
- When Gmail sending is available and Bhavin explicitly asks to send, send from **bhavinrtailor@gmail.com** after the final recipient, subject and body have been approved.
- Never auto-send discovered opportunities. Discovery and drafting remain separate from sending.
- User reviews every message before it is sent.
